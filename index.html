Above Par-adowski Mini-Golf, Par-t 2

WebXR putt putt from prototype to polished feature built for the Meta Quest 2 browser.

This game started out as a 40-hour WebXR prototype, but now aspires to compete in quality with native VR apps. I blogged about this process here.

This game is designed to use one Quest 2 controller. Select left or right-handed settings before beginning. Use the joystick to teleport manually, or use the trigger to teleport right to the ball. The club will adjust to your height. Your score will be visible on your wrist watch. Enter VR to start.

-- James C. Kane.
