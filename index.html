A WebXR mini-golf game
made for virtual reality.

This game began as a 40-hour prototype made for Paradowski Creative's putt putt tourney, but quickly took on a life of its own. Our aim is native-quality VR gameplay & design on the 3D open web.

Read our case study for more.

How to play

This is a WebXR game
meant to be played in virtual reality.

or

Once the game begins,
press any circle button on either
controller to change your club hand.

Use the joystick to navigate manually
or trigger to teleport right to the ball.
The club will adjust to your height.

Sink your putt in as few strokes as
possible. Par or under is the goal.
